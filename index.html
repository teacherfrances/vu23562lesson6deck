Face, eyes and voice

When you talk with the teacher, show you are interested.
Look at the teacher
Smile
Nod
Voice up and down
Open arms
Try it: your teacher talks about a place. Listen with your face, eyes and head. No words!
Term 4  ·  Explore community services
Teach each ring: model looking at a student, smiling, nodding, a flat voice and then a voice that goes up and down, crossed arms and then open arms. Then talk for 30 seconds about a place you like. Students listen with face, eyes and head only. Praise good nods and eye contact. Be sensitive: in some cultures, looking at a teacher's eyes is not polite. Say: in an Australian conversation, eye contact shows you are interested and honest. You can look away for a moment, then look back. This previews Stop 7.
